Made slowly, by the people who sign it.

  • One watchmaker per movement

    The same hands assemble, regulate, and case each calibre, and their initials are engraved on the mainplate.

  • Regulated in five positions

    Every movement is timed dial up, dial down, and crown in three directions before it leaves the bench.

  • Serviced at the atelier

    Oriel watches return to the bench where they were built for cleaning, oiling, and regulation.
