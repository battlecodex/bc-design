Notes that stay useful after the meeting

Everything a team learns lands in one place, with the source one click away.

  • Quotes keep their source

    Every highlight links back to the moment in the interview, so a claim in a readout is always one click from its evidence.

  • Decisions have a history

    Log what the team decided, who decided it, and which notes it rests on. Reopen it when new evidence arrives.

  • Search reads like a colleague

    Ask what customers said about onboarding and get the three interviews that answer it, not a list of keyword matches.
